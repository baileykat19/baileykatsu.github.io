About

Queer.
Japanese-American.
Boston.

Bailey Katsu is a singer-songwriter whose solo project lives at the intersection of pop-punk energy and raw emotional honesty. Her music navigates the euphoria and anxiety of queer identity — unapologetically and without a filter.

She is also the lead singer of The Femmes, an all-women and nonbinary band performing throughout Boston, New York, and beyond.
